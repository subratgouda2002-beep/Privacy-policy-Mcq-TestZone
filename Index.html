Welcome to MCQ Test Zone. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles information when you use our app to practice central and state competitive examination quizzes.

1. Information We Collect

MCQ Test Zone does not require account registration, login credentials, or personal profile creation. We do not directly collect your name, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

2. Local Progress and Score Storage

To provide progress tracking features such as Attempted Tests and Recent Attempts history, the application stores quiz attempt records locally on your device using Android's Room SQLite database and DataStore Preferences.

Your Control: Locally stored progress data remains on your device. You can erase saved attempt counts and test history by opening the Profile/Score tab and selecting Reset Score, or by uninstalling the application.

3. In-App WebView and External Quiz Content

When you select a quiz card, MCQ Test Zone opens the corresponding practice page using an in-app browser (WebView) from our website:

https://www.mcqtestzone.in/

Standard web browser information, including temporary cache, cookies, and website analytics, may be processed when loading these pages. The website and its services may have their own privacy practices.

4. Google AdMob and Advertising ID

MCQ Test Zone uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and help keep practice tests free for students. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain device and diagnostic information, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (AD_ID).
  • Approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Device information and Android operating system version.
  • Advertising impressions, clicks, and performance metrics.
  • Advertising and fraud prevention signals.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information

You can manage advertising personalization and Advertising ID settings through your Android device's privacy settings. Available options may vary by Android version.

5. App Permissions

MCQ Test Zone uses the following Android permissions for its core functionality and advertising services:

  • INTERNET: Required to load online quizzes and advertisements.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check network connectivity.
  • AD_ID: Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising-related services.

6. Sharing Features

When you tap the Share button, Android's native sharing interface allows you to voluntarily share an invitation link through applications of your choice. MCQ Test Zone does not read or store your contact lists or recipient details through this feature.

7. Data Security

The application uses HTTPS connections for supported online services. Local quiz progress is stored in the application's private storage on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

MCQ Test Zone is designed for competitive examination aspirants and general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through a linked webpage, please contact us so we can investigate and address the concern.

9. Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party services such as Google AdMob and external quiz webpages. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect application updates, changes in services, or applicable legal requirements. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.
